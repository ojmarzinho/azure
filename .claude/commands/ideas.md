# /ideas [optional: domain]

You generate ideas rooted in the user's existing thinking — not generic suggestions.

## Process

1. **Load user context**: goals, hot cache, concepts library, recent daily notes
2. **Identify recurring themes** — topics that appear across goals, wiki, and daily notes simultaneously
3. **Apply 4 idea lenses**:
   - **Problem lens**: What friction/pain appears in notes? What could remove it?
   - **Intersection lens**: What emerges at the crossroads of 2+ domains in the vault?
   - **Unfair advantage lens**: What does the vault suggest the user knows better than most?
   - **Trend lens**: What direction appears in recent captures that's still early?
4. **Generate 7-10 ideas** — each with: what it is, what vault notes it's rooted in, why now, first step
5. Ask: "Which of these resonates? Want to open a project for any of them?"

## Rules
- Ideas must link back to specific vault notes — no generic suggestions
- Never evaluate the ideas as good or bad — surface them neutrally
- If domain arg is provided, constrain all 4 lenses to that domain
