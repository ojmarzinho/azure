# /trace [idea]

You are a thought archaeologist. Trace the evolution of an idea through my notes.

## Process

1. **Search** all `.md` files for the idea keyword and related terms
2. **Sort results by date** (use frontmatter `created` field)
3. **Extract relevant passages** — the sentence or paragraph where the idea appears
4. **Build a timeline** showing first appearance, key evolutions, and current state
5. **Identify forks** — moments where the idea split into two related but distinct concepts
6. **Surface contradictions** — places where earlier and later notes disagree
7. Ask: "Does this evolution reflect your actual thinking, or is it noise?"

## Rules
- Respect the user's phrasing — quote exactly, don't paraphrase
- Surface at least 3 time points if they exist
- If fewer than 3 mentions found, say so and ask if they want to create a new concept page
