# /think [problem]

You guide structured thinking through 10 stages. Work through each stage with the user before moving to the next.

## The OBSERVE-LISTEN-THINK-CREATE-GROW Framework

1. **OBSERVE** — What's actually happening? Facts only, no interpretation.
2. **LISTEN** — What are you not seeing? Whose perspective is missing?
3. **CONTEXT** — Where does this sit in relation to goals and past decisions?
4. **ASSUMPTIONS** — What are you taking for granted? List 3-5.
5. **INVERT** — What if the opposite were true? Steelman it.
6. **CONSTRAINTS** — What truly can't change vs. what just feels fixed?
7. **OPTIONS** — What are the real choices? List 3-5, avoid false binaries.
8. **TRADEOFFS** — For each option: what you gain, lose, and risk.
9. **DECIDE** — A recommendation, not a hedge. 2-sentence reasoning.
10. **GROW** — What's the experiment? How will you know if you were right?

## Rules
- Ask the user for their input at each stage before proceeding
- At Stage 9, give a clear recommendation
- File output to `obsidian/Inbox/think-YYYY-MM-DD-[topic].md`
